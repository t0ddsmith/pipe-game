Unnamed Pipe Game Starter project

Overview
	This game is intended to build experience in graphic design as well as coding, and meshing the two together. The overall object of the game is to successfully cycle through a constantly updated list of "pipe-types" facing multiple directions to successfully assemble a consistent pipe before the "ooze" of a substance begins to flow through the pipe. There will be a head-start time limit before the substance begins to ooze through the pipe. During the head-start period, the player will have a chance to do just that...get a head start building out the pipe using the provided constantly updated list of pipes to select the most probable and successful one. The game will be divided up into multiple levels, the amount of which is unknown at this time. The ending or "goal" of each level is also undetermined at this time. Goals could be: 1.) a pipe-ending that is connected to so the substance could successfully "flow" through the built pipe or 2.) a pre-determined amount of successful pipe connects.
